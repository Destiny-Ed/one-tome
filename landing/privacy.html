Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

OneTome (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a local-first reading application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the OneTome mobile app and this website.

1. Information we collect

OneTome is designed to work primarily on your device. We do not require an account and we do not collect personal information through the app for advertising or tracking.

  • Books and reading data — PDFs you import, reading progress, highlights, notes, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device using the app’s private storage and SharedPreferences. This data is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Optional notifications — If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. We do not receive or store notification content.
  • Analytics / crash reports — The current version of OneTome does not include third-party analytics or crash reporting SDKs. If we add them in the future, this policy will be updated.

2. Permissions

The app requests the following device permissions only when needed:

  • File / storage access — To let you pick and import a PDF book from your device or Files app.
  • Notifications — To send optional daily reading reminders (you can disable this at any time in Settings).

3. How we use information

Because reading data stays on your device, we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties. The app uses the data solely to provide the one-book reading experience (progress, goals, highlights, streaks).

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or reading data with third parties. Exporting notes or highlights is done locally by you; we never receive those files.

5. Data retention and deletion

All app data remains on your device until you delete the app or use the in-app option to clear data. Deleting the app removes local books, progress, and settings from the device.

6. Children’s privacy

OneTome is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
