Questions

Quick answers before you start.

Do I need an account?

No. OneTome is local-first. Your books, progress, and notes stay on your device.

Can I read physical books?

Yes. Add a physical book with title and total pages, then log your current page and notes as you read.

What happens if I want to start a second book?

You can’t — until you finish the first one. That’s the point.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works without an internet connection.

Can I export my notes?

Yes. Highlights and notes can be copied or exported from the reader.
