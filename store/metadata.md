## Google Play

### Short description (80 characters max)

One book at a time. Finish what you start. PDF or physical.

### Full description (4000 characters max)

OneTome is a local-first reading app that helps you finish one book at a time.

Most reading apps let you open endless books and never finish any. OneTome enforces a simple rule: only one active book. You can’t start a second until the first is done.

Whether you’re reading a PDF or tracking a physical hard copy you already own, OneTome keeps you focused with daily goals, streaks, notes, and a clear finish line.

FEATURES

• One book rule — only one active book until you finish it  
• PDF or physical — import a PDF or track a hard copy with title, pages, and optional cover  
• Daily reading goal — set pages per day and mark “done for today”  
• Streaks — keep your reading habit alive  
• Highlights & notes — select text in PDFs or add free-form notes for physical books. Export anytime  
• Hard-copy feel — page-curl turns and night-shift filter for PDFs  
• Local-first — your books and notes stay on your device. No account required. Works offline  
• Celebration — finish a book and get a full-screen confetti moment

HOW IT WORKS

1. Choose your book (PDF or physical)  
2. Read every day and hit your goal  
3. Finish it, celebrate, then start the next one

OneTome is built for people who want to stop collecting unfinished books and actually finish what they start.

One book. Finish it.
